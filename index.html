Why we're going

A day and a half away to do two things we rarely make time for: set the direction for the year ahead, and get to know each other beyond the boardroom table.

We start with a normal group session at Mick's in Rockingham, then head down the coast for dinner and a night by the water. On Friday each of us builds and tests a one-page strategy for 2027, sets personal goals, and leaves with commitments the group will hold us to.

Built around your working week

Both mornings are yours. Work until lunchtime on Thursday, then from your room on Friday before we start at 10. The fun stuff sits around the core, and all of it is optional.

Good to know

Before you pack

Getting there

  • Perth to Rockingham: ~45 min
  • Rockingham to Mandurah: ~30 min
  • Mandurah to Perth: ~55 min
  • Car-pooling welcome. Tell us in the RSVP.

Weather

  • Early February is peak summer, typically around 30°C
  • Calm mornings, sea breeze in the afternoon
  • Sunset around 7:15pm

What to bring

  • Laptop for your two working mornings
  • Swimmers, hat and sunscreen
  • Smart casual for dinner
  • Your completed pre-work

Pre-work

  • The CEO strategy guide and a personal goals sheet arrive in January
  • About an hour to draft your one-page 2027 strategy before you come
